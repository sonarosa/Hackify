# 🩺 Hackify  
**Automated Medical Assistant & Smart Chatbot**  
🏆 **First Prize – Hackify conducted at Mar Athansius College of Engineering ** 
---

## 📜 Overview
**Hackify** is an **AI-powered medical assistant** designed to support **junior doctors** and **medical interns** in three critical areas:  
1. **Medical image interpretation** (X-rays, CT scans, MRIs)  
2. **Evidence-based treatment planning**  
3. **Smart document-based query answering**  

Our goal is to bridge the knowledge gap between early-career physicians and senior specialists by providing **fast, accurate, and context-aware assistance** — improving diagnosis accuracy, treatment outcomes, and workflow efficiency in clinical settings.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement
Junior doctors and medical residents often face:
- Difficulty interpreting **complex medical images** under time pressure.
- Reliance on **senior doctors** for confirmation of diagnoses and treatment plans.
- Delays in patient care due to **workload bottlenecks** and knowledge access barriers.
- Limited access to **specialized expertise** in real-time.

These challenges can lead to:
- **Misdiagnosis** or delayed diagnosis for chronic and acute conditions.
- **Treatment delays** impacting patient recovery.
- Increased **burden on senior physicians**.

---

## 🚑 Our Solution
Hackify acts as a **digital co-pilot for junior doctors**, offering:
- **AI-powered image analysis** for detecting abnormalities in X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs.
- **Contextual treatment planning** recommendations aligned with medical best practices.
- **Document-based smart chatbot** for querying uploaded medical guidelines, research papers, or case studies.

By integrating **computer vision**, **natural language processing**, and **knowledge retrieval**, Hackify empowers junior doctors to make **faster, more confident decisions** — reducing dependence on senior physicians for routine cases.

---

## 🛠️ Features

### 🖼️ AI Medical Image Interpretation
- Detects and classifies **diseases or abnormalities** using YOLO-based models.
- Supports multiple modalities: **X-ray**, **CT scan**, **MRI**.
- Provides **confidence scores** and highlights areas of concern.

### 📋 Treatment Planning Assistance
- Suggests **evidence-based treatment protocols**.
- Tailors recommendations to **patient context** and disease stage.
- Includes cross-checks with medical guidelines.

### 💬 Smart Document-Based Chatbot
- Allows users to **upload medical documents** (guidelines, papers, case reports).
- Uses NLP to answer **specific queries** based on the uploaded materials.
- Supports multi-turn conversation for detailed guidance.
